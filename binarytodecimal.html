🔵 Decimal to Binary Tricks

Trick 1: The "Powers of 2" Table Method (Fastest for Mental Math)

Yeh sabse best trick hai. Pehle 2 ki powers yaad kar lein: 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1

Example: Convert 13 to Binary.

1286432168421
00001101

Kaise karein?

  • 13 me se 8 fit hota hai? Haan (1 likho). Bacha: 13 - 8 = 5.
  • 5 me se 4 fit hota hai? Haan (1 likho). Bacha: 5 - 4 = 1.
  • 1 me se 2 fit hota hai? Nahi (0 likho).
  • 1 me se 1 fit hota hai? Haan (1 likho). Bacha: 0.

Answer: 00001101

Trick 2: Division by 2 Method (Standard Method)

Number ko 2 se divide karte jao aur Remainder (Bach) ko neeche se upar padho.

Example: Convert 13

DivisionQuotientRemainder (Read Bottom to Top ⬆️)
13 ÷ 261
6 ÷ 230
3 ÷ 211
1 ÷ 201

Answer: 1101

🟢 Binary to Decimal Tricks

Trick 1: Positional Weight Method (Standard)

Har bit ko uski position ki power of 2 se multiply karo (Right se Left: $2^0, 2^1, 2^2...$)

Example: Convert 1101

Binary1101
Weight ($2^n$)$2^3$ (8)$2^2$ (4)$2^1$ (2)$2^0$ (1)
Multiply1 × 8 = 81 × 4 = 40 × 2 = 01 × 1 = 1

Total: 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 13

Trick 2: The "Doubling" Method (Super Fast Mental Trick 🧠)

Isme kisi power ya table ki zarurat nahi. Left se Right jao, pichle answer ko 2 se multiply karo aur current bit add karo.

Example: Convert 1101

  1. Start with 0
  2. 0 × 2 + 1 (first bit) = 1
  3. 1 × 2 + 1 (second bit) = 3
  4. 3 × 2 + 0 (third bit) = 6
  5. 6 × 2 + 1 (fourth bit) = 13
💡 Pro Tip: Yeh trick bade binary numbers (jaise 10110101) ko dimaag me solve karne ke liye sabse best hai!
